# Product Warranty Terms

## 1. Warranty Period

All SINIAN LAB products come with the following warranty periods from the date of purchase:

- **Portable Speakers**: 1 year warranty
- **Headphones**: 1 year warranty
- **Multi-Room Speakers**: 2 years warranty

The warranty period begins from the date of first purchase and activation of the product.

## 2. Warranty Coverage

During the warranty period, we will provide free repair or replacement services if the product has quality issues due to the following reasons:

- Manufacturing defects
- Material quality issues
- Workmanship problems
- Functional failures due to non-human damage

## 3. What is NOT Covered

The following situations are NOT covered by the warranty:

- Damage caused by accidents, misuse, abuse, negligence, or abnormal use
- Failures caused by not operating according to the product manual
- Damage caused by natural disasters, fire, floods, or other force majeure
- Normal wear and tear, scratches, discoloration, or other signs of normal use
- Damage caused by self-disassembly, modification, or repairs by unauthorized service providers
- Product serial numbers that have been altered, damaged, or are unrecognizable

## 4. Warranty Service Process

To apply for warranty service, please follow these steps:

1. **Contact Customer Service**
   - Email: support@sinianlab.com
   - Service Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM

2. **Provide Purchase Proof**
   - Purchase invoice or receipt
   - Product serial number (located on product packaging or body)

3. **Problem Description**
   - Detailed description of the product issue
   - Provide photos or videos of the problem (if applicable)

4. **Review and Processing**
   - We will review your application within 3 business days
   - After approval, we will arrange for repair or replacement service

## 5. Repair or Replacement

Depending on the specific situation, we may provide the following services:

- **Repair**: Free repair of the faulty product
- **Replacement**: If repair is not possible, provide a replacement of the same model or equivalent value
- **Refund**: In special cases, such as when the product is discontinued and cannot be replaced, a refund may be requested

We bear all shipping costs for warranty services.
