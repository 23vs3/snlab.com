# SNILAB After-Sales Policy

SNILAB products sold strictly follow the "Consumer Rights Protection Law of the People's Republic of China" and the "Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China" for after-sales service, with the following service contents:

## I. Return and Exchange Policy

### 1. No-Reason Return

Customers who purchase products through SNILAB official online store, or official flagship stores on Taobao/Xiaohongshu and other platforms, may apply for a no-reason return within 7 days from the day after receipt on the same platform where the product was purchased. Customized products and products with explicit platform-specific instructions do not support this policy.

When returning products, customers must provide valid proof of purchase and return the invoice.

Customers must ensure that returned products maintain their original quality and functionality, with intact appearance, original accessories without defects, and complete trademarks and various identification marks on the product itself and accessories. If there are gifts, they must be returned together. Gifts include physical items, points, vouchers, coupons, etc. If gifts cannot be returned together, SNILAB may require customers to pay the gift price as previously indicated, and SNILAB has the right to directly deduct this amount from the refund.

If the product shows human damage, missing packaging box, or missing parts, return will not be processed.

Logistics costs for returns shall be borne by the customer.

Refund of the paid amount will be processed within 7 days from receipt of the returned product. The refund amount is the actual amount paid by the customer (excluding coupons, discount vouchers, membership cards, etc.). The refund method is the same as the payment method. The specific arrival date may be affected by banks, payment institutions, and other factors.

After return, all rights and benefits enjoyed by the customer when registering the product will be terminated.

### 2. Performance Fault Return

Within 7 days from the day after receipt, if a non-human-caused performance fault occurs, after confirmation by SNILAB after-sales service center, SNILAB will process the return. When returning, customers must provide valid proof of purchase and return the invoice. If there are gifts, they must be returned together.

### 3. Performance Fault Exchange

Within 15 days from the day after receipt, if a non-human-caused performance fault occurs, after confirmation by SNILAB after-sales service center, SNILAB will process an exchange, replacing the complete product. After exchange, the product's warranty period will be recalculated. For customized products and products with explicit platform-specific instructions, in such cases, they will be replaced with the same model standard products, excluding customized services or other accessory services.

## II. Warranty Policy

Within one year from the day after receipt, if a non-human-caused performance fault occurs, after confirmation by SNILAB after-sales service center, SNILAB will provide repair service.

SNILAB has the right to inspect products applying for warranty and perform repairs based on actual conditions, or in cases where repair is not possible, use parts that are equivalent to new products in performance and reliability to replace the parts that need repair. For customized products in such cases, the replaced parts will not retain the original product's customized content.

### Warranty Time Determination Standard

When applying for warranty service, customers must provide valid proof of purchase. If customers cannot provide valid proof, the warranty start date will be based on the manufacturing date shown in the product's serial number (SN), extended by 30 days.

## III. Cases Not Covered by Warranty Policy

The following situations are not covered by warranty:

1. Products purchased through unauthorized channels, or when valid proof of purchase cannot be provided.

2. Missing, altered, or inconsistent product serial numbers with registered service products.

3. Product faults caused by human damage, decorative or non-functional damage, normal performance degradation over time, and natural wear of consumable parts, such as: deformation, glue separation, paint peeling, scratches, wear, natural shell cracking, body corrosion, noise, battery degradation, etc.

4. Experience perception differences caused by personal preferences, usage habits, or experience (excluding faults explicitly stated in our written documentation), including but not limited to appearance assembly tolerances, appearance color, machine feel, sound color issues, volume size, sound clarity, noise level, machine operation smoothness, personal operation familiarity, heating, battery life, etc.

5. Catastrophic product damage, including but not limited to perforation, crushing, missing parts, separation, etc.

6. Damage or malfunction of additional accessories and consumables, such as chargers, data cables, silicone ear tips, protective cases, etc.

7. Loss of any information or data added on the basis of original factory configuration, as well as any indirect losses.

8. Damage caused by failure to install, use, maintain, or store according to instructions, such as liquid immersion, component corrosion, insect damage, animal bites, etc.

9. Product loss, theft, or damage/faults caused by theft, burglary, robbery, etc.

10. Temporary performance impact, degradation, or irreversible damage caused by external environmental interference, such as sound interruption caused by strong signal interference environments, performance damage caused by strong electromagnetic environments, etc.

11. Performance degradation, damage, or irreversible damage caused by force majeure (such as fires, earthquakes, floods, extreme weather, public energy interruption, etc.).

12. Loss, damage, or faults caused by war, terrorism, hostile acts, strikes, riots, civil unrest.

13. Faults occurring in any commercial use environment or business premises or in non-household or non-personal use situations, such as speakers used in hotels, cafes, and other venues; speakers or headphones used for trial listening or trial wearing in shopping malls or stores. The definition of commercial use and business premises shall be based on our judgment.

14. Faults or damage caused by unauthorized disassembly, modification, or repairs by unauthorized service providers.

## IV. Consumer Responsibilities and Obligations for Warranty Services

1. Before service, please back up personal data or information in advance, and delete sensitive or important data in advance.

2. When we provide services to you, we may need to verify your identity. We hope you understand and actively cooperate. If you do not provide true and complete information, preventing us from verifying your identity, you may not be able to enjoy the service.

3. When we provide services to you, we will inquire about relevant information about you and service guarantee subjects. Please answer truthfully. If you fail to answer truthfully and cause losses to us, we have the right to recover losses from you. If fraudulent behavior is found, we reserve the right to pursue legal action.

## V. Special Circumstances During Warranty Period

During the product warranty period, if products cannot be normally warranted due to the following reasons, including but not limited to: no stock of official repair parts, and no replaceable products of the same specifications, etc., after negotiation and agreement between the customer and SNILAB, return can be processed, and depreciation fees will be charged for returned products.

Depreciation fees are calculated from the invoice date to the return date, excluding the time occupied by repairs.

### Calculation Formula

**Depreciation Fee = Depreciation Rate (daily) × Product Usage Days × Product Price**

SNILAB product depreciation rate is **0.3% daily**, and the product price is based on the actual selling price shown in valid sales documents.

This rule only applies to products purchased from SNILAB official online stores*, and customers must hold valid invoices or valid proof of purchase.

*Official online stores include: SNILAB Flagship Store, Xiaohongshu SNILAB Flagship Store, Amazon SNILAB Flagship Store.

## VI. Paid Repair Service

For products with human damage or faults beyond the warranty period, SNILAB provides paid repair (or paid replacement in lieu of repair) services.

Fees will be quoted by SNILAB after-sales service center after inspection and evaluation based on the actual damage condition of the product. The final price is subject to SNILAB customer service quotation.

For products or parts after paid repair, the warranty period starts from the day after the customer receives the product, with a warranty period of **90 days**.

After paid replacement in lieu of repair service for customized products, the original product's customized content will not be retained.

SNILAB reserves the right to update, modify, and interpret after-sales terms. Implementation standards are based on the latest version published online. For purchase behaviors that occurred before the effective date of this rule, the rules at that time apply. For purchase behaviors that occurred after the effective date of this rule, this rule applies.
